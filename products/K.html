K.

Eau de Parfum · 100 ml

Sweet, dark and strangely addictive. Not confectionery sweetness, but something warmer, deeper and harder to leave alone.

K. is about the person you keep wanting back. The kind of presence that lingers after it leaves — familiar enough to crave, dark enough to remain slightly out of reach.

Lemon opens the composition with light before white musk, dark chocolate and Indonesian patchouli pull it somewhere more intimate. Vanilla and sandalwood soften the darkness without making it innocent.
